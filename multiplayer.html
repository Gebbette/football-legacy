THE WHOLE GAME

Four steps

  1. Join. One friend creates a room and shares the five-character code.
  2. Play the career. Make the same style of career decisions as Quick Story, including transfer choices.
  3. Keep it secret. Current-season stats and points stay hidden while everyone plays.
  4. Reveal and race. Watch the cinematic roll through every scoring category, reveal the season winner, then see totals tick upward as the overall leaderboard reorders. Highest total after ten seasons wins.

The host keeps the page open. After the final, the same connected party can start another career instantly with no new code.
