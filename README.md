# Football Legacy

**Football Legacy is a free browser football career simulator.** Start as a young player, develop your career, move clubs, win trophies and individual awards, and see what legacy you can build.

## Play Football Legacy

**Live game:** https://footballlegacy.co.uk/

No download or account is required. Career saves stay on the player's device.

## Game modes

- **Quick Story** — a fast, narrative football career designed for complete careers in roughly 6–10 minutes.  
  https://footballlegacy.co.uk/quick-story.html
- **Career Express** — month-by-month progression with more player development, transfer and football detail.  
  https://footballlegacy.co.uk/express.html
- **Original Career** — the deepest Football Legacy mode, with the fullest match, squad, lifestyle, transfer and long-term career systems.  
  https://footballlegacy.co.uk/original.html
- **Play With Friends** — multiplayer career competition with shared points reveals and final legacy comparisons.  
  https://footballlegacy.co.uk/multiplayer.html

## What you can do

Build a football career across clubs and competitions, develop your player, make career decisions, earn transfers, chase major trophies and individual awards, and compare the final legacy of different careers.

Football Legacy is an independent, unofficial football simulation project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any football club, league, federation, competition organiser or award organiser.
