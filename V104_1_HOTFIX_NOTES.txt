Football Legacy V104.2 — Season Review reliability fix

Fixed the end-of-season review so it no longer depends on hidden radio-button CSS to render correctly.

Changes:
- The Season Review is always the default visible panel.
- Ballon d’Or top-100 seasons use explicit tab buttons with reliable JavaScript switching.
- The continue/back action now sits outside the tab panels, so it remains available on both tabs.
- Non-top-100 seasons always render the normal review with no hidden-panel dependency.
- Review rendering is more defensive around missing stats/honour values.
- The same fix is applied to Quick Story and multiplayer, which share the review component.

No season simulation, transfer, development, award probability, multiplayer scoring or career-state logic was changed.
