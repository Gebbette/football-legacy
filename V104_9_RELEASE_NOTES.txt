FOOTBALL LEGACY V104.9 — REALISM + MULTIPLAYER RECOVERY
========================================================

QUICK STORY / MULTIPLAYER REALISM
- Rebuilt trophy probability around club reputation, division/tier and competition strength.
- Removed the old elite-player override that could drag modest clubs to titles too easily.
- Lower-division clubs now have genuinely rare odds of winning major domestic cups.
  Example calibration: a 95-rated star at Wycombe produces roughly a 0.05% FA Cup win chance per season, not several percent.
- Champions League wins require top-flight qualification and a credible club platform.
- Club/league awards remain possible at lower levels, but global awards are gated by top-flight and elite competition credibility.
- The Best FIFA Men's Player now requires a genuine Ballon d'Or-level season (normally top 3; top 5 only with major UCL/international success).
- Ballon d'Or voting now applies competition and club-platform credibility.
- HARD RULE: second-tier and lower players cannot enter the Ballon d'Or top 100, regardless of raw G/A.
- Low-reputation top-flight clubs can still produce major individual seasons, but world-ranking ceilings require major UCL/international achievements.

MULTIPLAYER RECOVERY
- Protocol moved to V13 to prevent stale older clients mixing with the new recovery model.
- Player identity now uses both a reconnect token and a persistent device identity.
- A refreshed/restarted client can reclaim its saved slot even if its temporary PeerJS identity changed.
- If the reconnect token changes, the host can still match the saved device; as a final fallback it can match one uniquely disconnected footballer name.
- Disconnected player slots are reserved for 10 minutes instead of being dropped quickly.
- Presence timeout is more tolerant of phones sleeping/backgrounding.
- Host room snapshots survive refresh/restart; host restoration retries temporary signalling/ID failures rather than deleting the party.
- Host recovery now has its own large RESTORE ROOM & PLAYERS screen.
- Returning clients get a large RECONNECT TO MY PLAYER screen.
- Presence/focus/pageshow/online recovery paths all reassert the saved slot.

PLAY AGAIN FIX
- Fixed the stale localCareerReady state that caused a second multiplayer game to skip setup and fall into Quick Story.
- Play Again now fully resets the career/reveal gates for game two and later.
- Temporarily disconnected friends are not silently removed when the host starts another game; their reserved slots remain available to reclaim.

PRESENTATION
- Skip Animation is now a clear high-contrast pill button with explicit “Reveal award now” copy.
- Cache versions bumped so browsers do not keep the older multiplayer/cinema scripts after deployment.
