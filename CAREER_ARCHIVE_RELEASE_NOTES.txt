FOOTBALL LEGACY — LEGACY RECORDS V1
===================================

WHAT'S NEW
- New home-page tab: Legacy Records.
- Completed careers are saved on the player's own device using localStorage.
- Best career score is tracked separately for Quick Story, Career Express and Original Career.
- Overall records include total careers, total seasons, apps, goals, assists, trophies and awards.
- Personal records include highest OVR, most goals, most assists, most trophies and longest career.
- Recent career history is shown with mode, club, score and career totals.
- Existing completed saves still present on the device are imported automatically where possible.

SAVE SAFETY
- Existing active career save keys are not changed or deleted.
- Legacy Records uses its own new storage key: footballLegacy_career_archive_v1
- Career records survive refreshes/browser restarts on the same device unless the player clears site/browser storage.

FILES TO REPLACE/ADD IN THE WEBSITE ROOT
- index.html
- quick-story.html
- express.html
- original.html
- career-history.js (new)
- career-history.css (new)

No multiplayer files are changed by this update.
