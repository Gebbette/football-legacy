FOOTBALL LEGACY — DECISION EXPANSION V1
=======================================

Base build:
Football_Legacy_Legacy_Records_V1_1_SCORE_FIX.zip

Quick Story decision update:
- Added all 60 requested new decision scenarios.
- Decisions are context-aware, so situations appear when they make sense for age, role, reputation, club status, position and career stage.
- Added a recent-decision memory so the same scenarios are not constantly repeated.

Decisions now affect real simulation systems rather than only changing text:
- transfer probability and number of offers
- appearances / selection odds
- match-performance odds and volatility
- development / OVR growth
- reputation
- club/team-result odds
- captaincy probability
- international-call-up probability
- award probability

Transfer example:
- Strong 'push for a move' choices materially increase the likelihood of a real offer and can create a hotter market.
- Strong 'commit to staying' choices materially suppress transfer offers.
- The game still keeps performance, age, club level, budgets and realistic candidate clubs involved, so decisions alter probability rather than guaranteeing an impossible transfer.

Decision consequences:
- The following season now explicitly reports the chosen action and its direct mechanical effects.
- Outcomes still include uncertainty, so a risky decision can pay off and a sensible one can occasionally fail.

QA performed:
- JavaScript syntax validation passed.
- 60/60 added decision IDs verified.
- 390px mobile-width decision layout checked with no horizontal overflow.
- 50 full Quick Story careers simulated with 252 expanded/normal decisions and no failed retirements or page errors.
- Controlled transfer test showed strong stay decisions producing far fewer offers than neutral choices, while transfer-pushing decisions produced more offers.
