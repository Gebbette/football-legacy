About

The idea behind Football Legacy and how the first public website is structured.

What is Football Legacy?

Football Legacy is a browser-based football career simulator built around one idea: start with an unknown young player and see what kind of career they can create.

The three modes

  • Original Career — the deepest and longest version.
  • Career Express — the middle version, with instant month-by-month simulation.
  • Quick Story — the fastest narrative version for shorter repeat careers.

Privacy-first V1

The first public version deliberately avoids accounts, advertising and analytics. This keeps the game fast, simple and more private while it is being tested with real players.
