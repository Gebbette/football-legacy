FOOTBALL LEGACY V104.3 — ACHIEVEMENT-BASED BALLON D’OR

Changed across Original, Express, Quick Story and Multiplayer:
- Ballon d’Or voting no longer directly uses OVR.
- Quick Story / Multiplayer top-100 ranking and the actual winner now use the SAME achievement calculation.
- Goals and assists are adjusted by position, so midfielders, defenders and goalkeepers are not judged like strikers.
- Match rating, appearances, trophies and individual honours all contribute.
- World Cup, Champions League and league titles receive prestige weighting.
- Direct OVR gates were removed from major individual honours that feed the Ballon d’Or race.
- 95+ OVR no longer receives an automatic extra World XI / The Best / Ballon d’Or boost.
- Historic seasons no longer contain an OVR-based Ballon d’Or shortcut.

OVR still affects how capable a player is during match/season simulation, so a stronger player may achieve more. OVR itself is not a Ballon d’Or voting input.
